How to send the PDF to your computer

Email or AirDrop work best. (Sharing to Slack from a phone does not attach the file reliably.)

iPhone

  1. Tap Export PDF. In Safari, tap Download when asked. In Chrome, tap Open in… on the bar that appears and skip to step 3.
  2. Tap the download icon or page menu at the left of the address bar, then Downloads, then badges.pdf. (It is also in the Files app, under Downloads.)
  3. Tap Share (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  4. AirDrop (to a Mac nearby): tap AirDrop, then your Mac. It lands in the Mac's Downloads folder. The Mac needs to be awake, with AirDrop on.
    Email: tap Mail, Gmail or Outlook. The PDF is already attached; add the address and send.

Android

  1. Tap Export PDF. When Downloaded appears at the bottom of the screen, tap Open. (Missed it? Chrome menu ⋮ → Downloads → badges.pdf, or the Files app → Downloads.)
  2. In the PDF viewer, tap Share (or ⋮ → Share).
  3. Tap Gmail or your mail app. The PDF is attached; add the address and send.
